Water Softener and Filtration in Menlo Park, CA

Before you buy a softener, find out whether your water needs one. In most of Menlo Park it does not.

Water softener and filtration service in Menlo Park, CA starts with a question: what problem are you trying to solve? Hardness, chlorine taste, sediment and specific contaminants each call for a different system. Local water is soft by most standards, so a salt softener is often unnecessary, while a carbon filter or reverse osmosis unit may suit people who dislike the taste of chlorinated water.

We install filters, under-sink reverse osmosis units and softeners when they make sense. Call (650) 860-1670 and tell us what you notice at the tap.

How Hard Is Menlo Park Water?

Hetch Hetchy water, which supplies much of the area, averages about 60 ppm hardness as calcium carbonate. California Water Service reports a lower 46 ppm in its own system. Under about 60 ppm, water is generally classed as soft, which means scale and soap film are less of an issue than in much of California.

Your own hardness depends on which provider serves you and any blending. Menlo Park Municipal Water, California Water Service and O'Connor Tract Co-operative Water serve different parts of the city, and each publishes a water quality report you can read.

When a Softener Is Not Worth It

Adding sodium and spending on salt for water that is already soft often brings little benefit. Softeners also discharge brine to the sewer, and some jurisdictions restrict or discourage that. Check local rules and your sewer district before buying.

If you have noticed scale, check whether it comes from a water heater, a hard water source such as a well or a different supply than you assumed. Then decide.

A Note on Claims

Filtration marketing can promise a lot. We do not claim a system removes anything it is not rated for. Look for third-party certification marks on the specific contaminant you care about, and keep your filter changes on time, because an old cartridge can make water worse.
