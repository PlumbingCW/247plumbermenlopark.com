Tankless Water Heater Service in Menlo Park, CA

On-demand hot water works best when it is sized, vented and flushed correctly. We cover all three.

A tankless water heater in Menlo Park, CA heats water as it moves through the unit instead of storing it in a tank. That gives endless hot water at a steady flow and frees floor space, but the unit needs enough gas or electric supply, a proper vent or circuit and regular flushing. We install, repair and flush gas and electric tankless heaters.

If your tankless unit shows an error code or runs cold, call (650) 860-1670. Many problems come from scale, a clogged filter or a flow rate that is too low to start the burner.

Why Tankless Units Need Flushing

Minerals in water settle on the heat exchanger and narrow its passages. Scale reduces efficiency and can set off error codes. Flushing means circulating a descaling solution through the unit, which removes buildup. Most makers recommend it at regular intervals.

Our local water is soft, with hardness averaging about 60 ppm as calcium carbonate in the Hetch Hetchy supply, and 46 ppm in California Water Service data. That slows scale compared with hard water regions, so flushing intervals can be longer, but the manufacturer's schedule and your own water source still decide the plan.

Common Tankless Problems

Cold water sandwich

A burst of cold water between hot bursts happens when the unit shuts off at low flow and restarts.

Error codes

Codes point to ignition, venting, flow or temperature problems. The manual or the plumber can read them.

Low flow

A clogged inlet screen or scale can reduce flow below the minimum.

Venting trouble

Blocked or loose venting can trip safety shutoffs.

Unit never fires

Power, gas supply or a flame sensor may be at fault.

Switching From a Tank to Tankless

Replacing a tank with a tankless model often needs a larger gas line, a new vent and sometimes a new electrical circuit. Condensing models use plastic venting and a condensate drain. Electric models need heavy wiring and may need a panel upgrade. We assess each of these before quoting the job.

Homes from the 1950s in the Willows, Belle Haven and West Menlo often have a garage or exterior wall that can hold a wall-mounted unit. Downtown and Allied Arts homes may need creative placement.

Gas Safety Notes

Tankless gas units move a lot of gas, so supply sizing matters. If you smell gas at any time, leave the building, then call 911 and PG&E at 1-800-743-5000 from outside. Do not turn any switch or try to relight the unit.

Safety checklist for gas tankless units

  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 and PG&E at 1-800-743-5000 from outside.
  • Do not relight or reset a unit that keeps shutting off on a safety fault.
  • Keep the vent and the air intake clear of storage and debris.
  • Never block or cap the condensate drain or the pressure relief line.
  • Turn off power and gas before any maintenance.
