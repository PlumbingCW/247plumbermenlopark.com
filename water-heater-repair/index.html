Emergency Water Heater Repair in Menlo Park, CA

Cold showers and a puddle under the tank are both reasons to call. We tell you on the phone whether it is a repair or a replacement.

Water heater repair in Menlo Park, CA means finding why a tank or tankless unit has stopped heating, started leaking or begun making noise, and fixing the part that failed. A pilot or thermocouple, a heating element, a thermostat, a gas valve and a drain valve are the usual suspects. A leak from the tank body is a different story, because the tank cannot be repaired.

If water is coming from the tank itself, close the cold water valve above it, turn off gas or power and call (650) 860-1670. If there is no hot water and no leak, check the breaker or pilot before the visit.

No Hot Water: What to Check First

  1. Electric heater

    Check the breaker and the reset button on the upper thermostat. A tripped breaker that trips again points to a failed element.

  2. Gas heater

    Look at the pilot or the display. If the pilot will not stay lit, the thermocouple or gas valve may be failing.

  3. Check for leaks

    Look under and around the tank. A wet floor changes the call from repair to emergency.

  4. Check the temperature setting

    A thermostat set too low makes the water feel lukewarm.

  5. Call if it will not start

    Do not keep relighting a pilot that goes out. It may be a safety shutoff doing its job.

Emergency Water Heater Repair at Night

A leaking water heater is the urgent version of this call. Water can flood a closet or garage quickly, and the heater itself may be damaged beyond repair. Close the cold water inlet, turn off the gas or the electric breaker and open a hot faucet to relieve pressure. Then call the 24/7 line.

A heater with no leak and no hot water can often be diagnosed the same day. The plumber brings common parts and tells you before replacing anything whether the repair is worth the money.

Gas, Electric and Hybrid Heaters

Gas units use a burner, a gas valve and a flue. Electric tanks use two elements and two thermostats. Hybrid heat pump models use a compressor and a fan on top of an electric tank and need clear space around them. Each has its own failure points, and the plumber tells you which part is at fault before ordering anything.

Tankless units are covered on our tankless water heater page. Heat pump models have condensate lines and air filters that need attention, and the plumber can show you how to keep them clear.

Local Notes on Menlo Park Heaters

Water here is soft. The Hetch Hetchy supply that reaches much of the city averages about 60 ppm hardness as calcium carbonate in the regional water report, and California Water Service reports a lower figure of 46 ppm for its own system. Soft water means less scale on elements and the tank floor than in much of California, so heaters that fail early here more often fail from age or anode depletion than from sediment.

Check the anode rod and the temperature and pressure relief valve with a plumber at regular intervals. A heater in a garage or closet may also need seismic bracing, so ask the Building Division what applies and have the plumber confirm yours is strapped.
