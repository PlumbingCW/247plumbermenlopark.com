Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Menlo Park, CA

Every minute a pipe runs, more water gets into the house. Shut off the main, then call. We send a plumber who can stop the water and repair the pipe.

Burst pipe repair in Menlo Park, CA is the work of finding a split or broken section of supply pipe, cutting it out and replacing it so pressure holds. Pipes burst from corrosion, pinhole failures that finally open, a hard water hammer or a joint that gives way. Most water in a burst comes from the supply side, so the first job is always stopping the flow at the main.

While you wait, close the main valve, open a low faucet to drain the line and move valuables away from the water. Then call (650) 860-1670. The dispatcher will ask a few questions so the plumber arrives with the right parts.

Do This Before the Plumber Arrives

  1. Close the main

    Turn the main valve clockwise at the front of the house, in the garage or at the meter box. If it is stuck or you cannot find it, call us and we will talk you through the next valve to try.

  2. Cut power if it is wet

    If water is near outlets, switch off that circuit at the panel only if the panel itself is dry.

  3. Drain the lines

    Open a faucet at the lowest point of the house to relieve pressure.

  4. Protect belongings

    Lift furniture, move rugs and photograph everything for your insurer.

  5. Call the 24/7 line

    Describe where the water is coming from and which valves are already closed.

Emergency Burst Pipe Repair, Any Hour

Burst pipes do not follow a schedule. We answer the line around the clock and put active water ahead of planned visits. A plumber can usually isolate the broken section, repair it and restore water to the rest of the house in a single visit when parts and access allow.

If the pipe is inside a wall, the plumber opens the smallest section needed, repairs the break and tells you what the drywall will need afterward. Rebuilding the wall is a separate trade, and your insurance company can point you to restoration help.

Why Pipes Burst in Menlo Park Homes

Age is the main reason here. Menlo Park's peak home building ran from the 1930s in Allied Arts and Felton Gables to the 1950s in Belle Haven, the Willows and West Menlo. Copper and galvanized supply lines from those decades suffer from corrosion that thins the wall until it splits, often at a joint.

Freezing is rare on the Peninsula, so the common cause is not ice. It is a pinhole that finally opens, a surge in pressure or a mechanical failure in a fitting. Sharon Heights sits on hillside lots where pressure at a fixture can run high, and a pressure check tells you whether a regulator is worth adding.

Finding the Break Before Opening Walls

If you cannot see the water, a plumber listens at fixtures and shutoff valves, reads the meter with every fixture off and uses acoustic and thermal tools to locate the leak. That limits drywall damage. Our leak detection page explains the tools in more detail.

A meter that keeps spinning with everything off is the clearest sign of a hidden break, and slab homes may show warm spots on the floor. See slab leak repair if you feel one.
