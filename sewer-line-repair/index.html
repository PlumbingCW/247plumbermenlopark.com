Emergency Sewer Line Repair in Menlo Park, CA

A cracked or collapsed sewer line cannot wait. We find the failure on camera, explain your repair options and give a clear price before digging.

Sewer line repair in Menlo Park, CA fixes the pipe that carries waste from the house to the sewer main. The usual causes are tree roots working into joints, cracked clay or cast iron, bellies where the pipe has settled and full collapses. The plumber confirms the problem with a camera, then proposes a repair that suits the damage and the yard.

If sewage is coming up in the house or standing in the yard, stop running water and call (650) 860-1670. Calls with sewage inside are placed first.

Signs the Sewer Line Itself Is Failing

  • More than one drain backs up at once, especially the lowest in the house.
  • A drain clog returns within weeks of being cleared.
  • Soggy ground, a sinking patch of lawn or sewer smell near the line.
  • A gurgle from toilets when the washer or tub drains.
  • Sewage showing at the cleanout.

Emergency Sewer Line Backup

A full backup is a health hazard and a damage risk. Stop using water, keep people away from standing sewage and call the 24/7 line. The plumber clears the line first if that is safe, then runs a camera to find the cause, so you are not told to dig until the problem is known.

If you can reach the cleanout, leave it closed unless the plumber asks you to open it. Opening it can release sewage into the yard.

Cast Iron, Clay and Why Older Laterals Fail

Cast iron was common in sewer and drain lines until plastic took over. It corrodes from the inside, develops rough scale and can crack at hubs. Clay pipe is joined in short sections, so every joint is a place for roots to enter. Both materials are common in houses built before the 1960s, which covers much of Allied Arts, Felton Gables and Belle Haven.

A cast iron pipe that is rough but intact can often be jetted or lined. One with holes or sagging sections is replaced. A camera view makes the difference clear.

Who Owns What in Menlo Park

Most of Menlo Park is served by West Bay Sanitary District, with East Palo Alto Sanitary District and Fair Oaks Sewer Maintenance District covering small areas. The district maintains the main in the street. The lateral between the house and that main is typically on the property side, though the rules vary by district and by lot, so ask your district where its responsibility begins.

Permit and inspection rules for lateral repairs depend on the district and the city. The plumber can explain what applies to your address.

Before You Dig, Get the Camera View

A sewer camera inspection shows the pipe type, the location of roots or breaks and the length of damage. It keeps repairs targeted and it gives you a recording for the file. If you are buying or selling a house, the same footage helps both sides understand what the line needs.
