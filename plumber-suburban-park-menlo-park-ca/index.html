Plumber in Suburban Park, Menlo Park, CA

Plumbing help for Suburban Park and the neighboring Lorelei Manor and Flood Triangle, around Bay Road and Flood Park.

Suburban Park sits on the northern edge of Menlo Park around Bay Road, Highway 101 and Flood Park. The city's documents treat Lorelei Manor and the Flood Triangle as neighboring areas, and so does this page. See our Menlo Park plumbing page for the whole city.

The city describes tree-lined streets, well-kept homes close together and medium-sized lots. If you need a plumber for a leak or a drain, call (650) 860-1670.

Ranch Homes and Flat-Roof Contemporaries

Ranch houses and flat-roofed contemporary homes can hide plumbing in different places. As general information, a slab foundation puts supply and drain lines under the floor, while a raised foundation leaves them reachable from a crawl space.

We do not know which applies to your house until we look. If a hot spot appears on a floor or the meter spins, start with slab leak repair and leak detection.

Flood Park and Nearby References

Flood Park is a 21-acre county park on part of the old James C. Flood estate, and its headquarters at 215 Bay Road is a landmark. Bright Angel Montessori Academy sits beside a single neighborhood commercial parcel.

Drivers know the area from Bay Road and Marsh Road. A nearby cross street helps us place your call.

Storm Drainage and Heavy Rain

Menlo Park noted on December 31, 2022 that Haven Avenue at Marsh Road was affected by the Atherton Channel during that storm. We have not confirmed how it affected Suburban Park streets, so treat this as context and not a claim about your street.

After heavy rain, watch for gurgling floor drains or slow fixtures. Stop using water and call. A sewer inspection shows whether the line is damaged.

Water Heaters and Drains

Many homes here date from the 1940s and 1950s, but the heater and fixtures in any home may have been replaced several times since. If a heater is leaking, see water heater replacement. For slow drains, see drain cleaning.

Whatever you have, we check the actual equipment before suggesting anything.
