Plumbing Services in Menlo Park, CA

Every service below is dispatched from Menlo Park and covers the nearby towns on our service area page.

Most plumbing problems start as one of three things: water where it should not be, water that will not go where it should, or water that is not hot. The pages below are organized around those symptoms and the work that fixes them.

Pages marked as emergency services come with 24/7 dispatch. Planned work such as a new water heater installation or a repipe is scheduled for a convenient day, and the price is explained first.

All Plumbing Services

Emergency Drain Cleaning

Slow sinks, backed up tubs and clogged main lines cleared with snakes, cameras and jets.

Emergency Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water scours grease, scale and roots out of main lines and branch drains.

Emergency Burst Pipe Repair

Water running from a split pipe is stopped, the pipe repaired and the system tested.

Emergency Slab Leak Repair

Leaks under the concrete found with listening tools and repaired by spot repair or rerouting.

Emergency Sewer Line Repair

Cracked, rooted or collapsed sewer laterals found on camera and repaired or replaced.

Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning

Main sewer lines cleared of roots, grease and paper so every drain in the house runs again.

Emergency Water Heater Repair

No hot water, leaks, pilot problems and noisy tanks diagnosed and repaired, gas or electric.

Emergency Garbage Disposal Repair

Jammed, leaking or dead disposals repaired or replaced, with the drain checked at the same time.

Emergency Toilet Repair

Leaking, running, clogged or rocking toilets repaired or replaced, with the flange and supply checked.

Emergency Leak Detection

Hidden leaks found with meter tests, acoustic gear and thermal imaging before walls are opened.

Water Heater Replacement

Failed tank removed and a new gas, electric or hybrid unit set, vented, strapped and tested.

Water Heater Installation

New water heaters planned for remodels, additions, upgrades and fuel switches, with permits handled.

Tankless Water Heater Service

Tankless water heaters installed, serviced, flushed and descaled for steady hot water.

Sewer Camera Inspection

Video inspection of the sewer lateral to find roots, cracks, bellies and breaks before any digging.

Commercial Plumbing

Restaurants, offices and multi-unit buildings served for leaks, drains, grease traps and backflow.

Faucet and Fixture Repair

Dripping faucets, leaking valves, weak showers and worn cartridges repaired or replaced.

Whole-House Repiping

Failing galvanized, copper or polybutylene supply lines replaced, with water service line options.

Plumbing Installation

New sinks, toilets, showers, dishwashers, disposals and hose bibs installed for remodels and upgrades.

Water Softener and Filtration

Honest advice and installation of filters, reverse osmosis and softeners for Menlo Park water.
