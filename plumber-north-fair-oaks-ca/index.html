Emergency Plumber North Fair Oaks CA Near Menlo Park

North Fair Oaks sits between Menlo Park, Atherton and Redwood City. We answer the 24/7 line for leaks, drains and sewer backups across the area.

ZIP codes we serve94063

North Fair Oaks, CA is an unincorporated area of San Mateo County, located between Redwood City, Atherton and Menlo Park. Because it is not a city, the County of San Mateo governs it, and the North Fair Oaks Community Council advises the Board of Supervisors. That affects permits and who to call about street-side problems.

If a pipe has failed, a drain is backing up or a water heater is leaking, call (650) 860-1670. The 24/7 line is open every hour, and calls with running water or sewage come first.

Emergency Plumbing Along Middlefield Road

The Middlefield Road Improvement Project is one of the county's listed projects for the area. Middlefield Road is a main route through North Fair Oaks and a major arterial in neighboring Menlo Park, so the dispatcher can often use it as a reference point when giving you an arrival window.

Homes and small businesses along and near the road call us for leaks, drains and heater failures. Our commercial plumbing page covers shops and restaurants.

ZIP 94063 and What It Covers

North Fair Oaks uses ZIP code 94063, which statistical area data puts at around 33,400 residents. The ZIP crosses jurisdiction lines, so some addresses are in unincorporated county land and some in Redwood City, which is why our Redwood City page also mentions it.

If you are not sure whether you are in the county or the city, ask the dispatcher or check a county map. Either way we serve the address.

Sewer Districts and County Public Works

The Fair Oaks Sewer Maintenance District works with San Mateo County and serves a small section of Haven Avenue in Menlo Park, according to the city's sewer page. We could not confirm from the county page which North Fair Oaks streets it covers, so call County Public Works if you are unsure who handles your main.

For the lateral and anything on your side of the property line, call us. A sewer camera inspection can show whether the problem is the lateral.

Community Plan, Older Homes and Remodels

The North Fair Oaks Community Plan guides growth, and the area sees remodels, additions and new units. Plumbing work in unincorporated land follows county permit rules, so ask the county before starting. Older homes often have original galvanized supply or clay drains that should be looked at when walls are open.
