Palo Alto homes range from 1890s cottages to 1950s Eichlers, and each needs a different first look. Call the 24/7 line from anywhere in town.

Palo Alto, CA is a charter city in Santa Clara County, incorporated April 16, 1894, with a 2020 census population of 68,572. For plumbing, the most important fact is the age of the houses. North of Oregon Expressway most homes were built in the first four decades of the twentieth century, and south of it homes were mostly built in the twenty years after World War II.

When a pipe fails in either part of town, call (650) 860-1670. The emergency line is answered 24/7, and calls with running water or sewage come first.

Eichler Homes and Slab Questions

Joseph Eichler built almost 3,000 homes in Palo Alto, many south of Oregon Expressway. Many Eichlers sit on slabs with radiant heating pipes in the concrete, and water lines may run through the slab as well. A warm spot on the floor or a meter that will not stop is worth a call.

Our slab leak repair page explains how leaks are located and repaired. If you do not know whether your house has a slab, ask the plumber on the phone.

City of Palo Alto Utilities: Who to Call for What

The City of Palo Alto Utilities provides water, gas, electric and wastewater service within city limits, which is different from neighboring towns that use separate providers. For a gas leak, leave the house and call 911, then the utility's dispatch number, (650) 329-2579, from outside. Do not call a plumber first.

For problems in the street or at the meter, call the utility. For the pipes on the house side and the lateral from the house to the main, our plumbers can help, and your utility can tell you where its responsibility ends.

San Francisquito Creek Along the City Edge

San Francisquito Creek forms Palo Alto's northern and western boundary and is the same creek that put Menlo Park and East Palo Alto on flood alert on December 31, 2022. Homes near the creek can see runoff and groundwater pressure in storms, which affects crawlspaces, sump areas and floor drains.

If water enters a crawlspace or a basement drain gurgles, call the line and read our drain cleaning page for what we can clear.

Neighborhoods and Main Streets We Reach

We serve Downtown around University Avenue, Professorville, Barron Park, College Terrace and the California Avenue area, plus homes along El Camino Real. El Camino Real runs through the city as State Route 82 and is the quickest route from our dispatch at 1300 El Camino Real in Menlo Park, though traffic changes the window.

Palo Alto has two Caltrain stations, one downtown and one at California Avenue, which can help you give a landmark on the call.
