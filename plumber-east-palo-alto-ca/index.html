East Palo Alto sits just across Highway 101 from Menlo Park's Belle Haven. We answer the emergency line 24/7 and send a plumber for floods, clogs and failed pipes.

Emergency plumbing in East Palo Alto, CA is shaped by water in more than one way. San Francisquito Creek forms the city's southern edge, the Bay is to the north and east and US-101 cuts through the south. When rain is heavy, the same storm that fills the creek also loads the drains in the neighborhoods nearby.

If water is entering a garage or a floor drain is backing up, call (650) 860-1670. The line is open 24/7, and we place active leaks and sewage backups ahead of planned jobs.

What Heavy Rain Does Here

On December 31, 2022, San Francisquito Creek overtopped its banks into a neighborhood on East Palo Alto's western edge. Water reached Woodland Avenue and nearby streets, swamped more than 20 cars and turned a parking garage into a muddy lake. Crews built a 3-foot sandbag wall afterward to protect the area in later storms.

The 1998 flood of record swamped 1,700 properties and caused more than $28 million in damage. Most residents in the 2022 flood area lacked flood insurance, according to local reporting, so protecting a home before a storm and acting fast after one matters.

Who Provides Water and Sewer Service

East Palo Alto Sanitary District handles sewer service in parts of the city, and its contact line is 650.853.3189, with offices at 1960 Tate Street. It generally overlaps with O'Connor Tract Co-operative Water Company in the area between Menalto and Euclid Avenues. Palo Alto Park Mutual Water Company serves small areas as well, so the bill in your hand is the quickest way to know who covers your address.

The district or water company handles the main and the meter. For anything on the house side, call us.

Older Pipes and Shared Walls

Much of the housing in East Palo Alto is older and many homes are rentals. Older galvanized and copper supply lines can fail without much warning, and rentals sometimes have deferred work. If a landlord is slow to act, tenants can still call us for an emergency shutoff and temporary repair, though who pays depends on the lease and the situation.

A burst pipe that floods a unit may need burst pipe repair and, after the water is stopped, drying by a restoration contractor.

Highway 101, University Avenue and Getting There

US-101 has ramps at University Avenue and Willow Road, and the Dumbarton Bridge approach starts in Menlo Park. Dispatch for East Palo Alto runs from 1300 El Camino Real in Menlo Park, which is close, but traffic on 101 and Willow Road changes arrival time, so the dispatcher gives you a window on the call.

East Palo Alto's single ZIP, 94303, is shared with part of Palo Alto, so give the street address on the call to avoid confusion.
