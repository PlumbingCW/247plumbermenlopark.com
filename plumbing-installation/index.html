Plumbing Installation in Menlo Park, CA

Whether it is a single fixture or a whole bathroom, we install it, connect it and test it before we go.

Plumbing installation in Menlo Park, CA covers adding or replacing fixtures and lines: toilets, sinks, tubs and showers, dishwashers, disposals, laundry boxes, hose bibs, ice maker lines and new supply and drain runs for remodels. The plumber checks existing lines, sets the fixture, connects supply and drain, and tests for leaks.

Permits and Inspections

New plumbing work often involves a permit and an inspection before the walls are closed, depending on the city and the scope. The Menlo Park Building Division can be reached at 650-330-6704, and neighboring cities have their own offices. We tell you what applies to your address.

Closing walls before inspection is a common mistake that costs time. Ask your contractor to schedule rough-in and final inspections.

Working With Other Trades

Plumbing is one piece of a remodel. The general contractor, tile setter and cabinet installer all affect the schedule. We coordinate on rough-in dates, fixture delivery and shutoff windows and finish the plumbing after the other trades are done.

Older Houses, New Fixtures

A new fixture on old pipe may uncover problems. In houses from the 1930s and 1950s, such as those in Allied Arts, Felton Gables, Belle Haven and the Willows, drain lines may be cast iron or clay and supply lines may be galvanized. We check what the new fixture connects to before installing, and we tell you if a section should be replaced while the wall is open.

See repiping for whole-house supply upgrades and sewer inspection for the drain side.

Kitchens, Baths and Laundry: What Each Needs

Kitchen

Sink, faucet, disposal, dishwasher and ice maker lines all tie into the same supply and drain. If you are adding an island sink, the drain needs slope and a vent, which can limit where it can go.

Bathroom

Toilets, vanities, tubs and showers each need a drain of the right size and a vent. Moving a toilet more than a short distance usually means opening the floor.

Laundry

A washer box needs supply valves and a standpipe drain. A drain pan and a shutoff valve are cheap protection against a hose failure upstairs.
