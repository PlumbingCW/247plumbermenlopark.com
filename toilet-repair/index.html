Emergency Toilet Repair in Menlo Park, CA

A toilet that overflows or leaks at the base needs attention now. A running toilet can wait for the next visit but wastes water every hour.

Toilet repair in Menlo Park, CA fixes the parts that fail most often: the flapper, the fill valve, the wax ring at the base, the supply line and the flange that holds the toilet to the floor. A bowl that will not flush, a tank that keeps refilling and water around the base each point to a different part, and the plumber can usually fix them in one visit.

If a toilet is overflowing, lift the tank lid and push the flapper closed, then shut the valve behind the toilet. Call (650) 860-1670 if the overflow was sewage or the floor is wet.

What Each Symptom Usually Means

Runs on and off

A worn flapper or a bad fill valve lets water leak from the tank into the bowl. It wastes water and shows up on the meter.

Water at the base

A failed wax ring or loose bolts let water seep out as you flush. The leak rots the floor over time.

Weak flush

Clogged rim jets, a partial clog or a low water level can cause a weak flush.

Rocking or loose

A loose toilet strains the wax ring and the supply line. Tighten the bolts or reset the toilet.

Gurgling or bubbles

Air in the line can mean a clog further down or a problem with the vent.

Emergency Overflow or Leak

Overflow comes from a clog in the toilet or in the drain it feeds. A plunger clears most toilet clogs. If the bowl rises again or other fixtures bubble, the blockage is deeper and the drain cleaning team takes over. We prioritize overflow and leak calls over routine visits.

A leak at the base needs the water shut off and the toilet dried. A damp subfloor can grow mold quickly, so do not leave it until next week.

The Flange: The Part Nobody Sees

The flange is the ring on the floor that the toilet bolts to. If it is cracked, rusted or sitting below the finished floor, the wax ring cannot seal, and the toilet leaks again after a reset. A repair ring or a new flange fixes it, and the plumber should check the flange any time a toilet is pulled.

Homes with old cast iron drain lines, as in 1930s and 1950s houses in Allied Arts, Felton Gables and Belle Haven, often have flanges that have rusted. A flange repair is cheaper than redoing the floor.

Water Waste and Your Bill

A toilet that runs silently can waste a lot of water over a month. If your water bill has crept up and you suspect a running toilet, add a few drops of food coloring to the tank and wait fifteen minutes. If color shows in the bowl, the flapper is leaking.

If the bill is high and the toilet is fine, ask about a meter check through our leak detection service.

Safety checklist for an overflowing or leaking toilet

  • Treat sewage water as contaminated and keep children and pets out.
  • Wear rubber gloves and wash your hands well afterward.
  • Turn off power to nearby outlets if water has reached them and the panel is dry.
  • Do not mix cleaning chemicals when disinfecting the floor.
  • Dry the area quickly, and photograph damage before cleanup.
