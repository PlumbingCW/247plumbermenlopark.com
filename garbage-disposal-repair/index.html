Emergency Garbage Disposal Repair in Menlo Park, CA

A dead disposal turns a full sink into a house problem. We diagnose it, repair it if it is worth saving and replace it when it is not.

Garbage disposal repair in Menlo Park, CA covers jams, motors that hum but will not spin, leaks at the seals or drain connections and units that stopped working entirely. Most of these problems are repairable in a visit, and the plumber also looks at the kitchen drain, because a slow line is often the real reason the disposal backs up.

A leak under the sink should be treated as urgent. Turn off the disposal switch and the water supply at the shutoff valves, then call (650) 860-1670.

Emergency Disposal Leaks and Jams

A leak from the bottom of the disposal cannot be patched, and the water runs into a cabinet that may hold cleaning supplies and wiring for a dishwasher. Switch off power at the wall, close the shutoff valves and call. We schedule leaks ahead of routine visits.

A jam is less urgent but can feel like a crisis during a dinner party or holiday. The plumber clears the jam, tests the unit and checks whether the motor shows wear.

Repair or Replace?

A jam, a worn flange gasket or a clogged drain pipe is a repair. A cracked housing, a seized motor or a disposal that trips the reset over and over is a replacement. When the unit is old and the repair price approaches half the cost of a new one, replacement is usually the better value.

If you are replacing, ask about motor size, noise insulation and whether the new unit fits your existing mounting. A plumber can install the new unit and reconnect the dishwasher drain in the same visit.

What Not to Put in a Disposal

  • Grease, oil and fat, which coat the line and cause backups.
  • Fibrous scraps such as celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves.
  • Coffee grounds in large amounts.
  • Bones, fruit pits and shells, which can jam the blades.
  • Rice, pasta and potato peels, which swell and form paste.

Kitchens in Older Menlo Park Homes

Kitchens in 1950s ranch houses in Belle Haven, the Willows and West Menlo often have a single branch line shared by the sink and dishwasher. If that line is partly blocked by years of grease, a new disposal will not fix a slow drain. The plumber runs a cable through the kitchen line while on site so the repair holds.

Remodels in the area sometimes add an island sink or a second dishwasher, and those lines tie into the same older pipe. Our plumbing installation page covers remodel work.

Safety checklist before anyone works on a disposal

  • Switch off the wall switch and the breaker, since a switch alone can be bumped back on.
  • Never put a hand or fingers into the grinding chamber, use tongs or pliers.
  • Close the supply shutoffs before opening any drain connection.
  • Keep water away from the outlet or junction box under the sink.
  • Call a plumber if the unit sparks, smokes or smells of burning.
