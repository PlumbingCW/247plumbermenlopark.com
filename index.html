Emergency Plumber in Menlo Park, CA, Any Hour

An emergency plumber earns the name at 2 a.m., not at noon. Menlo Park 24/7 Plumbing keeps the line open every night, weekend and holiday for burst pipes, sewage backups, failed water heaters and active leaks. A plumbing emergency is any problem that is damaging the home or making it unsafe to stay in right now.

Start by stopping the water. The main shutoff is usually at the front of the house near the hose bib, in the garage, or at the meter box by the street. Turn it clockwise until it stops, then open one faucet at the lowest point of the house to drain the lines.

Next, call (650) 860-1670. We ask a few direct questions, tell you what else to turn off, and book the fastest plumber we can send. Same-day appointments are offered when the schedule allows, and emergency calls are put first.

What the Age of Menlo Park Homes Means for Your Pipes

Menlo Park city planning records put peak home building in Belle Haven at 1950 to 1959, with ranch houses as the main type. Linfield Oaks, Suburban Park and Lorelei Manor follow the same mid-century pattern, and West Menlo added 833 homes in the 1950s alone. Pipes installed then are now more than 65 years old, so repeated pinhole leaks are worth taking seriously. Our leak detection visits start with the oldest lines in the house.

Allied Arts, where building peaked between 1926 and 1940, and Felton Gables, with most homes from the 1930s through the 1950s, tell a different story. Older sewer laterals under those streets are often clay or cast iron, which is why a camera look through sewer line repair planning pays off before a remodel. Sharon Heights grew through the 1950s, 1960s and 1970s, and the hills there raise water pressure questions that a pressure check answers in minutes.

Who Supplies Your Water and Sewer Service

The water meter sets the boundary of what the city or utility looks after. Menlo Park Municipal Water serves about 22,000 residents through roughly 4,400 connections, in an upper zone around Sharon Heights and a lower zone east of El Camino Real. California Water Service serves 8,798 Menlo Park addresses downtown and in West Menlo, and O'Connor Tract Co-operative Water serves a small section of the Willows.

Sewer service is mostly West Bay Sanitary District, with East Palo Alto Sanitary District and Fair Oaks Sewer Maintenance District covering smaller pockets. If water is gushing in the street or the sewer main is overflowing outside your property, call the utility on your bill. For anything on the house side, including the shutoff valve, the pipes inside and the lateral to the main, call us.

Winter Storms Find Every Weak Drain

On December 31, 2022, San Francisquito Creek and the Atherton Channel rose enough that Menlo Park posted a flood alert, and surface flooding reached El Camino Real, Alma Street, Laurel Street and Middlefield Road. KQED reported nearly 4 inches of rain that day and nine atmospheric river storms across California in three weeks. Storms like that load sewer laterals and floor drains at the same moment.

If a floor drain gurgles or a toilet bubbles during heavy rain, stop running water in the house and phone the emergency line. A backup after a storm is an emergency, and a sewer camera inspection afterward shows whether the pipe is cracked or just overwhelmed.
