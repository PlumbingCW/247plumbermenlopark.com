Emergency Hydro Jetting in Menlo Park, CA

When a cable only pokes a hole through the blockage, a jet cleans the whole pipe. We run jetting after a camera look so the pressure is right for your pipe.

Hydro jetting is a drain cleaning method that sends water through a hose and a nozzle at high pressure to scour the inside of a pipe. In Menlo Park, CA it is used for grease buildup, mineral scale, silt and soft roots in kitchen lines, main sewer lines and some commercial drains. The water cuts and flushes debris out of the pipe instead of just opening a channel through it.

Jetting is not for every pipe. A cracked clay or old cast iron line can be harmed by pressure, which is why a camera inspection comes first. If your main is backing up now, call (650) 860-1670 and we will tell you whether a jet or a cable is the right first step.

Signs a Main Line Needs Jetting

Repeat backups are the clearest sign. If a cable cleaned the line in spring and the drains slowed again by summer, the pipe wall is probably coated. Other signs include a gurgle from the lowest toilet when the washing machine drains, a slow kitchen line that clears briefly and returns, and a smell that does not leave after cleaning.

Restaurants and shared buildings see this faster than homes because grease and food waste move through the line all day. Our commercial plumbing page covers grease traps and scheduled line maintenance.

Emergency Hydro Jetting for Backed-Up Main Lines

A main line that backs up at night does not have to wait. We send a plumber who can run a camera, clear the line with a cable if that is the safe choice, and set up a jetting visit when the pipe condition allows it. Water pressure on the nozzle is set to suit the pipe material, not to a number on the machine.

When you call, tell the dispatcher whether sewage is already inside the house. That tells us how fast to move.

What Menlo Park Pipes Face

Sharon Heights, Linfield Oaks and the Willows all have homes built from the 1950s into the 1970s, and sewer laterals from that time were often clay, cast iron or early plastic. Joints in clay and cast iron let in fine roots, and cast iron develops rough scale on the inside. Jetting is useful on pipe in good shape, and a camera tells us before pressure goes in.

Sewage reaches the West Bay Sanitary District main in most of the city. Your lateral runs from the house to that main, so a line that keeps backing up may be an issue on the private side. Ask your district to confirm where the responsibility starts.

How a Jetting Visit Goes

  1. Camera first

    A small camera shows the pipe material, size and any breaks before pressure goes in.

  2. Access the line

    The plumber connects to a cleanout or pulls a fixture if no cleanout exists.

  3. Jet in passes

    The nozzle works forward and back along the pipe until the water returns clear.

  4. Camera after

    A second pass shows the cleaned pipe and confirms no damage was found.
