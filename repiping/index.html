Whole-House Repiping in Menlo Park, CA

Repiping is a big decision. We help you decide whether the pipes need replacing now or can be repaired for a few more years.

Repiping in Menlo Park, CA means replacing a home's supply lines, the pipes that bring water to faucets, showers, toilets and appliances. It is considered when pinhole leaks keep appearing, water turns rusty, pressure drops or a pipe material has a known failure record. The work usually involves opening walls in a few places, running new lines and patching access holes.

Not every leak means repiping. A single failure in sound pipe is a repair. If you are unsure, call (650) 860-1670 and ask for an honest assessment before you commit.

Signs Your Pipes May Be Near the End

Repeated pinhole leaks

A second or third leak in a few years means the pipe wall is thinning everywhere.

Rusty or brown water

Galvanized steel rusts from the inside and can discolor water.

Falling pressure

Corrosion builds up inside the pipe and narrows it.

Green stains

Blue-green staining on copper means it is corroding.

Known problem material

Polybutylene and some early plastics have a history of failure.

Pipe Materials: PEX, Copper and Others

PEX

Flexible plastic tubing that is quicker to install and resists corrosion. It needs protection from sunlight and from some chemicals. Check that the local code allows it for your use.

Copper

Durable and long-lived, but more expensive and more labor to install. Water chemistry can affect it over time.

Existing material

Galvanized steel, older copper and polybutylene are the usual types being replaced in older homes.

Water Service Lines: Meter to House

The service line runs from the water meter to the house and is the first pipe the water meets. Older homes may have galvanized, copper or lead-era materials, and a failing service line causes low pressure, wet spots in the yard and high bills. Whether a utility or the owner is responsible depends on where the leak sits relative to the meter.

Menlo Park Municipal Water, California Water Service and O'Connor Tract Co-operative Water each serve different parts of the city. Ask yours where its responsibility ends. Our leak detection service can locate a service line leak before any digging.

Homes Most Likely to Need It

Menlo Park's planning records show peak building from 1926 to 1940 in Allied Arts and from the 1930s to the 1950s in Felton Gables. Belle Haven peaked in the 1950s, and West Menlo added 833 homes in that decade. Houses that age may still have original supply pipe in places, or a mix of old and newer repairs.

A plumber can check pipe material in the garage, under sinks and at the meter, test pressure and tell you what the house has. Ask also about water pressure on hillside lots such as Sharon Heights.

What Living Through a Repipe Looks Like

The plumber opens small sections of drywall along the planned route, runs new lines and reconnects each fixture. Water is shut off for part of the day and restored before the crew leaves in most cases. Drywall patches are left rough, and painting is a separate trade.

Ask for a schedule, a plan for each bathroom and a list of access holes before the work starts.
