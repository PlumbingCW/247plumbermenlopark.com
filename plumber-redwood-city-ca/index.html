Emergency Plumber Redwood City CA Near Menlo Park

Redwood City is the county seat and a large place with distinct neighborhoods. Tell the dispatcher yours and we place the call right away.

ZIP codes we serve940619406294065

Redwood City, CA is the county seat of San Mateo County, a role it has held since the county formed in 1856. It had 84,292 residents in the 2020 census and stretches from the Bay toward the Santa Cruz Mountains, so a plumbing call here could come from a downtown condo, a Redwood Oaks bungalow or a house near the water.

For any failed pipe, backed-up drain or leaking heater, call (650) 860-1670. The 24/7 emergency line is answered at all hours, and Redwood City calls are placed by neighborhood so the plumber arrives knowing the area.

Neighborhoods We Serve in Redwood City

Downtown

Apartments, older commercial buildings and homes near the courthouse area. Older drains and shared lines are common.

Redwood Oaks and Roosevelt

Mid-century bungalows and ranch houses where clay or cast iron laterals may still be in the ground.

Friendly Acres and Mt. Carmel

Residential blocks with mixed ages of plumbing and frequent remodels.

Woodside Plaza and Centennial

A mix of single-family homes, with some newer repairs over older lines.

Palm and Redwood Shores

Newer and bay-adjacent construction, with different drainage and water table considerations.

Who Handles What for Water and Sewer

Utility boundaries in a city this large vary by address, and our research did not confirm a single provider for every neighborhood. California Water Service's Bear Gulch district reports serving portions of Redwood City, but we do not claim it covers all of it. Your water and sewer bills show the provider for your address.

For problems on the street side of the meter or main, call that provider. For anything on the house side, call us.

Downtown, El Camino Real and Woodside Road

El Camino Real and Woodside Road are the main roads for dispatch into Redwood City from Menlo Park. The city has a Caltrain stop and a port, the only deepwater port on San Francisco Bay south of San Francisco, which gives some drivers a landmark to give on the call.

Downtown businesses with grease lines, backflow devices or shared drains can read our commercial plumbing page. Atherton is next door, and its page is at Atherton.

Why a Short Drive Matters in an Emergency

Redwood City is a short drive up El Camino Real from our dispatch location, and distances in this area are small. Traffic on El Camino Real and US-101 changes arrival time, so the dispatcher gives a window instead of a promise.

If a pipe has already burst, closing the main and calling quickly makes the most difference. See burst pipe repair for what the plumber does on arrival.
