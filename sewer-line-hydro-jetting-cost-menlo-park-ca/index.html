Sewer Line Hydro Jetting Cost for Menlo Park, CA Homeowners

There is no single price for jetting. Here is what moves it up or down and how to read a quote.

By the Menlo Park 24/7 Plumbing team · 2026

The cost of hydro jetting a sewer line depends on the length of the line, how heavy the buildup is, how easy the access is and whether a camera inspection is included. A short, accessible lateral with moderate grease is a modest job. A long line with roots, no cleanout and a camera on each end is another.

Any firm number you see online is an average of very different jobs. The sections below explain what makes up a quote in Menlo Park, CA, so you can compare bids on equal terms. For a price on your own line, call (650) 860-1670 and describe the problem.

What Goes Into the Price

Factors that raise or lower a hydro jetting quote
FactorRaises the price whenLowers it when
Line length.Long run from house to the main.Short, direct lateral.

How Shops Price Jetting

Some firms charge a flat fee per visit, others charge by time or by the length cleaned. A flat fee is easy to compare but can hide limits on length. A time-based quote can rise if the buildup is heavy. Ask which method applies and what happens if the job runs long.

A fair quote says what is included, such as the camera, the number of passes, the access work and a written summary or video of the result.

Jetting Versus Cabling: Which Do You Need?

A motorized cable opens a path through a blockage and costs less. Jetting cleans the full pipe wall and costs more. A cable is usually enough for a one-off paper or toilet clog. Jetting makes sense for grease, scale and roots that keep coming back.

If you are not sure, our hydro jetting page explains when each method makes sense, and we will say so honestly if a cable is the right fix.

When Jetting Is Not the Right Call

  • The pipe is cracked, collapsed or heavily corroded, where pressure could do harm.
  • A camera shows a belly or break that cleaning will not solve.
  • The clog is a simple toilet blockage that a plunger or auger can clear.
  • The line has just been cleaned and is flowing well.
  • The sewer main in the street, not your lateral, is blocked.

Get the Camera Before You Pay for Jetting

A camera shows the pipe condition, the cause of the buildup and whether jetting is safe. It prevents paying for pressure on a broken pipe. It also gives you a recording to share with an insurer or a buyer.

See our sewer inspection page for what the video shows, and ask for the footage whichever plumber you choose.

Red Flags in a Jetting Quote

No camera

Without a camera, no one knows the pipe is safe to jet.

A flat number over the phone

A price without a look at the line may change once the plumber arrives.

Pressure to jet everything

A fair plumber recommends the least method that works.

No written scope

Ask for a short note on what is included.

Vague warranty

Ask how long the cleaning is expected to hold.

Questions to Ask Before You Book

  • Is a camera inspection included, and will I get the footage.
  • What pipe size and length does the quote cover.
  • What water pressure will be used and is it right for my pipe.
  • What happens if the line turns out to be damaged.
  • Will you give me a written scope and price before starting.

Jetting as Part of a Maintenance Plan

A restaurant or a rental property with a history of grease backups may do better with a scheduled jetting visit than an emergency call every few months. A set interval, set by what the first camera shows, spreads the cost and avoids a mess on a bad day.

A single-family home without a history of trouble does not need routine jetting. Jet when a camera shows buildup or after repeated slow drains, not on a calendar for its own sake.

What a Cheap Quote May Leave Out

Low prices sometimes leave out the camera, the access work or a second pass. That is not a scam, but it can leave you with a clean-looking result and no proof. Compare quotes line by line, not by the bottom number.

If a quote seems too good, ask what is excluded and what happens if the line backs up again within a week.
