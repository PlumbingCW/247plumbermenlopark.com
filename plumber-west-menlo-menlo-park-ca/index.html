Plumber in West Menlo, Menlo Park, CA

Plumbing help for the larger lots and mid-century and revival homes of West Menlo, between Valparaiso Avenue and San Francisquito Creek.

West Menlo is a large and varied residential area. The city's description bounds it by Valparaiso Avenue, Arbor Road, Vine Street and San Francisquito Creek, with tree-lined streets and rural sidewalk treatments. Start with our Menlo Park plumbing page for the overview.

Housing is mostly single-family, one to two stories, with Mid-Century Modern predominating alongside period revival homes. Counts show 205 homes before 1939, 360 in the 1940s and 833 in the 1950s. For help, call (650) 860-1670.

Large Lots, Long Lines

The city lists some of its largest residential parcels here, especially on San Mateo Drive, Robert S. Drive, Corinne Lane and The Hermosa Tract. As general information, a bigger property often means a longer sewer lateral from house to street.

A longer lateral has more joints and more length to dig if repair is needed. A sewer inspection finds the problem first, and spot repairs can be smaller than a full replacement. See sewer line repair.

Mid-Century and Period Revival Homes

West Menlo homes range from mid-century designs to Mediterranean, Tudor and Bungalow styles. The city lists several named examples along Arbor Road, Santa Cruz Avenue and Valparaiso Avenue.

Style tells you how a house looks, not what is in the walls. If supply lines need attention, repiping is one option after inspection.

Who Handles Sewer in West Menlo

West Bay Sanitary District lists Menlo Park among its service areas, and its office is at 500 Laurel Street in Menlo Park, phone (650) 321-0384. Your bill confirms who serves your address.

The district handles the main in the street. We handle your lateral and the plumbing inside.

Schools, Parks and Reference Points

The profiles list public and private schools, a church, a cemetery and an art gallery in the area, with Jack W. Lyle Park just outside the northwest end. Main streets include Valparaiso Avenue, Santa Cruz Avenue, Middle Avenue and Oak Knoll Lane.

Those references help us place a call. Your street address is still the key detail.

Flooding Near the Eastern Edge

The Housing Element profile notes that West Menlo's eastern border adjoins a flood hazard area. San Francisquito Creek ran high on December 31, 2022.

If water enters a crawl space or a drain backs up, stop using water and call. Drain cleaning only helps if the line itself is clear and undamaged.
