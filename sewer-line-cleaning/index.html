Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Menlo Park, CA

When every drain in the house is slow at once, the main line is the suspect. We clear it, look inside it and tell you what we saw.

Sewer line cleaning in Menlo Park, CA clears the main drain that carries all of a home's waste out to the street. It is different from clearing a single sink. A blockage in the main stops the whole house, and the lowest drain, often a shower or floor drain, is the first place sewage appears.

If you see sewage rising, stop using water at once and call (650) 860-1670. A plumber with a motorized cable, a camera and a jet can usually clear the line and show you why it clogged.

Why the Lowest Drain Overflows First

Waste flows downhill to the main, so when the main stops, the water has only one place to go: back up through the lowest opening in the house. That is why a basement shower, a floor drain or the ground floor tub shows sewage before upstairs fixtures do.

Stop flushing, skip the dishwasher and hold off on laundry until the line is clear.

What Blocks a Main Sewer Line

Tree roots

Fine roots enter through joints and grow into a mat that catches paper and waste. This is the most common cause in older clay and cast iron laterals.

Grease

Cooking fat that cooled in the line coats the wall and narrows the pipe until one more load closes it.

Wipes and paper

Products sold as flushable do not break up like toilet paper and catch on roots and rough pipe walls.

A belly in the pipe

A low spot holds water and solids. Cleaning helps for a while, but the section needs repair.

Broken pipe

A collapse looks like a clog. A camera tells the difference.

How Often Should a Main Line Be Cleaned?

Homes with no history of backups usually need nothing but good habits. A house with roots in the lateral may need cleaning every year or two until the pipe is repaired. Rental properties and shared buildings have more users on one line, so their schedule is shorter.

If cleaning is needed more than once a year, a camera inspection pays for itself. It shows whether the pipe can be saved or whether sewer line repair is the better use of money.
