Emergency Drain Cleaning in Menlo Park, CA

A drain that will not clear gets worse by the hour. Call the 24/7 line and we send a plumber with the tools to clear it and a clear price before work starts.

Drain cleaning in Menlo Park, CA means removing whatever blocks a pipe so water moves again. Depending on where the blockage sits, the plumber uses a hand auger, a motorized cable machine or a high-pressure water jet. A sink that stands full of gray water, a tub that fills while the shower runs and a toilet that gurgles when the washer drains all point to a clog deeper than a plunger can reach.

If sewage is coming up through a floor drain or the lowest tub in the house, stop using water and call (650) 860-1670. A single slow sink can wait for a scheduled visit, and the dispatcher will tell you which situation you are in.

Signs the Clog Is Past the Plunger

One fixture is slow

A single sink or tub that drains slowly usually has a clog in its own trap or branch line. This is the easiest case to clear.

Several fixtures are slow

When the shower, the bathroom sink and the toilet all drain badly together, the blockage is further down the shared branch or in the main line.

Gurgling and bubbles

Air pushed back through a trap means water cannot move forward. Gurgling after the washing machine runs is a classic main line sign.

Smell from a drain

A sewer odor that comes and goes can mean a dry trap, but a steady smell with slow flow suggests a partial blockage.

Water in the lowest drain

Sewage or gray water rising in a floor drain or basement shower means the main is blocked. Stop using water right away.

What Clogs Drains in Menlo Park Homes

Much of Menlo Park was built in the middle of the last century. The city's own planning records put peak building in Belle Haven at 1950 to 1959 and show Linfield Oaks, Suburban Park and Lorelei Manor following a similar mid-century pattern. Drain lines in houses that age have had decades to collect scale, grease and roots, so repeat clogs in the same pipe are common.

Older streets such as Allied Arts and Felton Gables, where many homes date from the 1930s, can still have clay or cast iron laterals. Roots work into the joints of those pipes, and a cable cleans the hole the roots made without fixing the crack. If a drain clogs more than once a year, ask for a camera look before paying for another cleaning.

Cable, Camera or Jet: How the Plumber Chooses

Hand auger and cable machine

A hand auger clears most sink and tub clogs in minutes. A motorized cable with a cutting head handles main lines and roots, and it is the first tool for most clogged drain calls.

Camera inspection

A small camera on a flexible rod shows what is blocking the line. It tells the plumber whether the cause is grease, roots, a belly in the pipe or a break, and it gives you a recorded view to keep.

High-pressure jetting

When the pipe walls are coated with grease or scale, a jet scours them clean in a way a cable cannot. See our hydro jetting page for what it can and cannot do.

Storm Drains, Area Drains and Cleanouts

Outdoor drains clog too. Patio and driveway area drains fill with leaves and silt, and a blocked one sends water toward the house wall. On December 31, 2022, surface flooding reached El Camino Real, Alma Street, Laurel Street and Middlefield Road after the San Francisquito Creek and the Atherton Channel rose, a reminder that runoff has nowhere to go when drains are already full.

We clear private storm drains, yard area drains and downspout lines that belong to the property. Street inlets and the public storm system belong to the city, so report those to Menlo Park Public Works instead.

What Not to Pour Down the Drain

  • Cooking grease and oil, which cool and coat the pipe wall.
  • Wipes sold as flushable, which do not break down like paper.
  • Coffee grounds and eggshells in the kitchen sink.
  • Hair in the shower without a catcher in the drain.
  • Chemical drain openers, which can sit in a blocked pipe and make the plumber's job harder.

What Drain Cleaning Costs and Why It Varies

The price depends on where the clog is and how hard it is to reach. A sink trap and a main line under a slab are different jobs. You get a clear price once the plumber has looked at the problem and before any work starts.
