Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Menlo Park, CA

A leak under concrete does not announce itself loudly, but it can empty a lot of water. We locate it first and then talk through repair options.

A slab leak is a leak in a water line that runs under or inside a concrete slab foundation. Slab leak repair in Menlo Park, CA starts with proving the leak is there and pinning down where. The plumber then decides between a spot repair through the slab, rerouting the line through walls or attic, or replacing the whole run.

Signs include a water meter that keeps moving with every fixture off, a floor that feels warm underfoot, sound of running water in silence and damp carpet at the edge of a room. If any of those fit, close the main and call (650) 860-1670.

Signs of a Leak Under the Floor

Meter keeps moving

Close every fixture and watch the meter. If the dial still turns, water is escaping somewhere between the meter and the house.

Warm spot on the floor

A hot water line leaking under the slab warms the concrete above it. Bare feet notice before eyes do.

Sound of water

A hiss or trickle at night when the house is quiet often means a pressurized leak.

Water bill climbs

A bill that jumps without any change in use is one of the most common first signs.

Damp flooring or baseboards

Moisture at the base of walls, lifting flooring or a musty smell points toward the area above the leak.

How Slab Leaks Are Located

The first step is a pressure test of the supply lines to confirm the leak, followed by electronic listening equipment that picks up the sound of water escaping a pressurized pipe. Thermal imaging can help on hot water lines. These tools narrow the search to a small area before anyone cuts concrete.

Our leak detection service uses the same methods, and the plumber will tell you honestly if the leak turns out not to be under the slab at all.

Emergency Slab Leak Calls

Call at any hour if the meter is spinning and you cannot find water, if a floor is wet or if a hot spot suddenly appeared. We schedule urgent leak calls ahead of planned work. Close the main in the meantime if you cannot find the source, and do not run the water heater until the leak is dealt with.
