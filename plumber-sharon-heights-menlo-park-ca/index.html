Plumber in Sharon Heights, Menlo Park, CA

Plumbing help for the larger homes, townhomes and condos around Sharon Park Drive and Sand Hill Road.

Sharon Heights is a younger Menlo Park neighborhood than the older areas near downtown. The city's profiles center it on Sharon Park Drive and describe a mix of single-family homes, planned developments and condos near Sand Hill Road. See our Menlo Park plumbing page for the whole city.

Construction in the city's counts runs 342 homes in the 1950s, 488 in the 1960s and 644 in the 1970s. Parcels are large and deep. For a leak, a heater problem or a drain, call (650) 860-1670.

Why Larger Lots Change the Plumbing Conversation

The city describes Sharon Heights lots as large, with deep and wide parcels. As general information, a larger property tends to mean longer runs of supply pipe and drain line, more outdoor faucets and sometimes more than one water heater.

More length means more places a leak can hide. A leak detection visit starts with the meter and works outward, so we are not opening walls on a guess.

Water Heaters in a Sharon Heights Home

Larger homes often have a bigger hot water demand. If yours is leaking, making noise or failing to keep up, see water heater repair or water heater replacement.

Some homeowners look at a tankless water heater when replacing a unit. We can talk through whether that fits your layout and gas or electric setup.

Landmarks and Local Reference Points

The profiles name Sharon Park, Sharon Heights Golf and Country Club and the Sharon Heights Shopping Center. They also list a public middle school, a private elementary school, offices and a school district office nearby.

The main streets are Sand Hill Road and Alameda de las Pulgas. These help us place a call, but your street address is what we need.

Condo and Townhome Water Shutoffs

In condos and planned developments, the shutoff for your unit may sit in a utility closet, a garage or a shared room. Ask your association where it is before an emergency.

If water is coming from a neighbor's unit or a wall you do not control, tell building management as well as us.
