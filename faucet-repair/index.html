Faucet Repair in Menlo Park, CA

A small drip is a slow leak, and a failed shower valve can be a bigger one. We fix the part that is failing and test the fixture before we leave.

Faucet repair in Menlo Park, CA covers kitchen and bathroom faucets, tub and shower valves, hose bibs and the shutoff valves under sinks. Most leaks come from a worn cartridge, a hardened washer or a corroded seat, and the plumber can usually replace the part in one visit. If the fixture is badly worn or no longer made, replacing the whole faucet may cost less than chasing parts.

If water is running out of control from under the sink or behind the wall, close the nearest shutoff or the main and call (650) 860-1670. A dripping faucet can wait for the next available visit.

Repair or Replace the Faucet?

If the faucet is less than about ten years old and the finish is sound, a new cartridge or washer is the sensible fix. If the body is corroded, the finish is pitted or parts are no longer available, a new faucet is better value. The plumber tells you which applies after taking a look and before ordering anything.

Kitchen faucets with pull-down sprayers and touchless controls have more parts that can fail. If the same faucet has been repaired twice in a year, replacement usually makes sense.

Shower and Tub Valves

A shower that drips for hours after use wastes water and can stain tile. The valve is behind the wall, and many are repaired by changing the cartridge through the trim. If the valve body is cracked or corroded, the wall must be opened.

Bathrooms in 1950s homes in Belle Haven, the Willows and Suburban Park sometimes have original galvanized supply lines behind the valve. If those are failing, see repiping.

Water Quality and Faucet Wear in Menlo Park

Soft water is gentle on faucets. The Hetch Hetchy supply averages about 60 ppm hardness as calcium carbonate, and California Water Service reports 46 ppm. That means less mineral buildup in aerators and cartridges than in hard water cities, but fixtures still wear from use and age.

Aerators can still clog with fine debris after street water main work. Unscrew the aerator, rinse it and put it back before calling us for a weak flow.

When a Fixture Leak Becomes an Urgent Call

Treat a leak as urgent if water is reaching a wall, a ceiling below or electrical outlets, or if you cannot shut it off. In that case close the main and call the 24/7 line. For hidden moisture see leak detection.
