Plumber in Belle Haven, Menlo Park, CA

Drain, leak, water heater and sewer help for houses and small buildings in Belle Haven, the Menlo Park neighborhood between US-101 and the Bay.

Belle Haven is the Menlo Park neighborhood east of US-101. The city's planning documents place it between Highway 101, Willow Road and the Dumbarton rail corridor. This page is for Belle Haven homeowners and landlords who want to know what we handle here. For the wider picture, see our Menlo Park plumbing page.

The city describes the area as mostly one-story single-family homes on compact parcels, with larger multi-family buildings along Hamilton Avenue, Willow Road and Pierce Street. Its housing counts show 292 houses built before 1939, 115 in the 1940s and 421 in the 1950s, the peak decade.

A build year tells you when a house went up. It does not tell you what is inside the walls, so we look at the actual pipes before recommending anything. To reach us, call (650) 860-1670.

Houses and Small Buildings on Compact Lots

With compact parcels, houses sit close together and the line from the house to the street is often shorter than on a large lot. That is general plumbing information, not a measurement of any Belle Haven street.

Buildings with several units often share a drain line, so one blockage can affect more than one household. If you manage a rental, tell us how many units are affected and where the water is showing. That shortens the first conversation.

Who Handles the Street Side

Menlo Park's sanitary sewer page names West Bay Sanitary District, East Palo Alto Sanitary District and the Fair Oaks Sewer Maintenance District as sewer providers in the city. Which one serves a given street is something we could not confirm from a street-level map, so check your bill or call the city. West Bay Sanitary District lists (650) 321-0384 and East Palo Alto Sanitary District lists 650.853.3189.

The provider handles the main in the street. The line on your side of the property is yours, and that is the part we work on.

Parks and Landmarks That Help Us Place You

Belle Haven has Kelly Park, Karl E. Clark Park and Hamilton Park, plus the neighborhood library and the Onetta Harris Community Center. When you call, naming a cross street or one of these places helps pin down the address quickly.

If a pipe has failed in a wall, a burst pipe repair visit starts with finding the shutoff, not with opening anything up.

Before You Call

  • Find the main water shutoff and close it if water is running.
  • Note which fixtures or rooms are affected.
  • Take photos if water has reached floors or walls, your insurer may ask.
  • Keep children and pets away from any sewage.
