Plumber in Allied Arts, Menlo Park, CA

Plumbing help for the older homes and tree-lined streets of Allied Arts, also called Stanford Park, near El Camino Real and San Francisquito Creek.

Allied Arts, also listed by the city as Allied Arts and Stanford Park, is one of Menlo Park's older neighborhoods. The city describes a grid of blocks with mature trees and small, older homes, bounded by El Camino Real, Middle Avenue and Creek Drive. The creek forms its eastern edge in the Housing Element profiles. Our Menlo Park plumbing page covers the city overall.

Parcels are consistent, long and narrow. Peak construction ran from 1926 to 1940, with period revival styles. For help, call (650) 860-1670.

Older Homes: Look First, Then Decide

A home from the 1920s or 1930s may have had pipes replaced, extended or rerouted over the decades. Its build year says nothing certain about what is installed today.

That is why we inspect before recommending. If supply lines turn out to be worn, repiping is one option, and a leak detection visit can tell you whether the problem is local or widespread.

Remodels and Additions

Older homes are often remodeled, and plumbing changes follow. New kitchens, added bathrooms and moved laundry rooms all call for plumbing installation work.

Permits for plumbing work in Menlo Park go through the Building Division, which lists 650-330-6704. Ask before you start so your plans match what the city expects.

Landmarks Near You

The Allied Arts Guild at 75 Arbor Road is a regional destination, and Capidro at 262 Princeton Road is a California Historical Landmark. Nealon Park is the neighborhood park, and Jack W. Lyle Park sits just outside the west side.

The Little House Activity Center and shops along El Camino Real are also nearby. A park or cross street helps us place your address on a call.

Tree Roots and Aging Drain Lines

Mature trees and narrow lots put root systems close to underground lines. This is a general risk on any street with big trees, and it does not mean your line is affected.

If drains are slow in more than one fixture, a sewer inspection can show whether roots, a belly or a joint is the cause.

Near the Creek

Allied Arts sits near San Francisquito Creek, which ran high on December 31, 2022, when the city issued a flood alert. After a wet storm, check basements, crawl spaces and drains for water or odors.

If anything backs up, stop using water and call.
