An emergency plumber in Atherton, CA has to deal with a particular kind of property. The town was incorporated on September 12, 1923, and is known for its one-acre minimum lot sizes, which means longer driveways, longer water service lines and sewer laterals that can run a long way from the house to the street.

A plumbing emergency on a big lot is easy to miss until it is large. If you see sewage, a wet lawn that is not from irrigation or a meter that will not stop moving, call (650) 860-1670. Long Laterals and Why They Matter Here

On a one-acre lot, the line from the house to the sewer main can be much longer than on a standard suburban lot. A longer line has more joints, more places for roots to enter and more length to dig if a section fails. Mature trees are a feature of Atherton, and their roots are a regular cause of repeat clogs.

Before digging, a sewer camera inspection shows where the problem sits. Spot repairs can be much smaller than a full replacement.

Who Serves Atherton for Water and Sewer

West Bay Sanitary District lists Atherton among the areas it serves for sewer, and its main office is at 500 Laurel Street in Menlo Park, phone (650) 321-0384. California Water Service's Bear Gulch district reports serving Atherton for water, with its purchased supply coming from the San Francisco Public Utilities Commission.

The utility handles the main in the street and the line up to the meter. The house side belongs to you, and that is where we work. Your bill shows which provider applies at your address.

Atherton Creek, Channel and Storm Water

The Atherton Channel and San Francisquito Creek both ran high on December 31, 2022, when Menlo Park issued a flood alert and noted that Haven Avenue at Marsh Road was affected by the Atherton Channel. Rain on that scale loads the sewer laterals and floor drains of homes nearby, and a backup after heavy rain is an emergency.

If a floor drain gurgles or a toilet bubbles during a storm, stop using water and call the line. Afterward, ask for a camera look so you know whether the lateral is cracked or just overwhelmed.

Older Estates, Eichler Pockets and Pool Houses

Atherton has estates, many from the early twentieth century, as well as the Lindenwood neighborhood in the northeast part of town, known for Eichler homes. Older estates often combine several generations of plumbing, from original supply lines to later additions. Pool houses and guest cottages add separate water heaters and drains that are easy to forget.

Holbrook-Palmer Park, a 22-acre public park, is a landmark in town. For repiping or an upgrade after a remodel, call and tell us which part of the property is affected.
