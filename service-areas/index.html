Plumber Service Areas Near Menlo Park, CA

Dispatch runs from 1300 El Camino Real in Menlo Park and reaches five nearby towns.

Menlo Park sits on the Peninsula between San Francisquito Creek and the Bayshore, and the towns around it share water sources, freeways and flood risk. We answer the emergency line for Menlo Park and five neighbors, and each page covers that town on its own terms.

Distances here are short. Atherton and East Palo Alto are the closest, and Redwood City and Palo Alto are a short drive up or down El Camino Real. Those are estimates and traffic on US-101 and El Camino Real changes them, so dispatch gives you an arrival window when you call.

Neighborhoods Inside Menlo Park

Inside the city we work in Belle Haven east of US-101, the Willows, Suburban Park, Lorelei Manor, Vintage Oaks, Felton Gables, Linfield Oaks, Park Forest, Allied Arts, Sharon Heights and downtown around Santa Cruz Avenue. The city lists each as a separate neighborhood, and the housing in each differs by decade, which changes what we check first. The home page explains the age patterns.

Atherton and the Creek Edge

Atherton sits just south and west of Menlo Park, with one-acre minimum lots and long laterals. The Atherton Channel and San Francisquito Creek both ran high in the December 2022 storm. See Atherton plumbing for sewer, leak and water heater calls on larger properties.

East Palo Alto Across US-101

East Palo Alto is a short drive from Belle Haven, with San Francisquito Creek on its southern edge. Water reached Woodland Avenue in the December 2022 flood, so storm drains and floor drains matter there. Read about East Palo Alto service before the next storm.

Palo Alto, in Santa Clara County

Palo Alto is the one town on this list in Santa Clara County, and it runs its own gas, water and wastewater utilities. Houses range from pre-1940 cottages in Professorville to Eichlers south of Oregon Expressway. See Palo Alto plumbing for what that means for pipes.

Redwood City and North Fair Oaks

Redwood City is the county seat, with neighborhoods from Downtown to Redwood Oaks. North Fair Oaks sits between Redwood City, Atherton and Menlo Park as an unincorporated county area, and permits there run through San Mateo County. Start with Redwood City or North Fair Oaks.

When the Problem Is Not on Your Side of the Meter

A water main break in the street, a sewer main overflowing at the curb and a gas smell outside are utility problems. Call the provider on your bill, or 911 for gas. Everything from the meter or the cleanout into the house, including the shutoff valve, the supply pipes, the fixtures and the sewer lateral, is where a plumber helps.

If you are unsure which side a leak is on, close your house main and watch the meter. Tell the dispatcher what you saw and we will sort out who should respond.

How Dispatch Handles a Call From Any of These Towns

Calls go to the same 24/7 line, and the dispatcher asks for your street, your ZIP code and what is happening. Active water and sewage are placed first, planned jobs are scheduled around them, and you hear a clear price before work starts.

Tell the dispatcher the nearest main road. Middlefield Road, Willow Road, El Camino Real and University Avenue are the reference points we use most for arrival windows.

Who Handles Water and Sewer Where

Palo Alto is the outlier. It sits in Santa Clara County and the City of Palo Alto Utilities provides its gas, water and wastewater service. The other towns use a mix of water companies and sanitary districts, so the bill in your hand is the fastest way to find who to call for a problem on the street side of the meter.
